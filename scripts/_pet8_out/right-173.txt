What is your advice to someone who wants

The most important thing is creating an
original story that develops in a way that
nobody can predict. The best thing about
Japanese manga stories is that they
are often set in a world apart. It's pure
imagination but at the same time the stories
follow a complex structure, so they are
suitable for adult audiences too, especially
