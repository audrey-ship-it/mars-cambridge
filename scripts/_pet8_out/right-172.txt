Interviewer
Today I'm here with the famous Japanese
manga creator Masayoshi Uwai and I'm
going to ask him a few questions about
his exciting job. How did you start drawing
manga?

Masayoshi
As long as | can remember, I've always
been fond of drawing. | come from a poor
family so we couldn't afford to own toys.
My grandfather used to bring us manga
that people threw away. | used to spend
hours reading the stories and copying the
drawings. Me and my sisters Kimiko and
Emiko used to create our own characters
and create new stories. Yeah, they were
just drawings but we actually played with
them as if they were dolls living in a real
place. Then | went to an art school. | had
worked during my high school to save money
for it, but | soon realised it wasn't suitable
for me so | left the course after a couple of
months and ended up teaching myself.

Interviewer
How did you become a professional manga
artist?

Masayoshi
| had created a long story which got positive
reviews from local editors so | decided
to take part in a contest. | won first prize
and in 1992 | officially started my career
with Cherry Blossoms in the Snow. It was
incredibly long series — about 45 volumes,
so it wasn’t always easy to develop the
plot. | still remember it as one of the most
enjoyable moments in my early career.
