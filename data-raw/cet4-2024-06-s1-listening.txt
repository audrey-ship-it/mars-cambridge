A team of about 25 firefighters spent about 25 minutes extinguishing the flames. News Report Two
A new study has cast doubt on historic research suggesting that the season or month of one's birth is associated with an increased risk of certain mental health conditions. The new study looks at symptoms of anxiety and depression among more than 70,000 older adults in Europe. [3] A number of past studies have found the link between season of birth and mental health diagnoses. Researchers have suggested that such links could arise from various things. These factors include nutrient intake, sun exposure, climate, and disease exposure in a year's time. However, the evidence has been mixed. More recent studies have suggested that such factors as social class or economic background have more to do with these mental health diagnoses than month of birth. [4] Overall, the new study found no significant relationship between participants' month of birth and symptoms of depression or anxiety. The study involved some 70,000 adults in Europe. It found that as people age, the risk factors for depression shift somewhat depending on birth month. People may place more value on social class or background factors than on their month of birth. Some countries reported slightly different seasonal patterns. Overall, no systematic pattern was found.

News Report Three
Genetic researchers in China have made a clone of a star police dog. The clone was born in December in a laboratory in Beijing. The cloned dog and its mother are almost identical genetically. The mother dog helped solve multiple murder cases and other serious crimes. [5] In multiple tests, the clone has already performed better than traditionally bred dogs. [6] If the clone continues to perform as well as expected, it could mean a huge reduction in the training time for police dogs, which usually takes five years. The ultimate goal of the scientists is to create police dogs that can be trained in months instead of years. [7] However, due to the current cost, this goal is not yet possible. It is not the first time a clone has been made of a star police dog. In South Korea, six cloned dogs began working with the police in 2008.

Passage Two
Imagine boating down the Amazon River, minding your own business—calmly keeping an eye out for alarmingly large snakes—and a curious pink dolphin appears to swim alongside. [19] While this may seem like a mythical creature, pink dolphins do exist in the Amazon region. The Amazon river dolphin is a giant among its species. It can measure up to 2 meters long and weigh about 204 kilograms. Size isn't the only thing that sets the Amazon river dolphin apart. Thriving in South American rivers and seasonal flood plains, this freshwater dolphin can even appear strikingly pink. Although born gray, males turn pink in adulthood, entering adulthood they will show distinct bright pink. [20] The unusual color is believed to be in fighting—either duels or competing for mates. The deeper the pink, the older the male, as the scar tissue accumulates. There is also a theory that this color helps dolphins blend into the environment more easily. During heavy rains, the Amazon rainforest rivers become pink, and it becomes easier to spot the pink dolphins. [21] Since 2018, this wetland system has been placed under international protection.
